# Practica Platform Effects MVP Playbook

## v1 Product Model

Practica v1 shipped as a private video practice product with trusted feedback flows.

The strongest shipped behavior is:

- a learner records or uploads a private take,
- that take lives in a private archive,
- the learner can generate a private review link,
- and a trusted person can respond with attached video feedback.

Even in v1, the emotional center is not a public audience.
It is a private mirror plus optional trusted response.

## Primary Flow

1. Record or upload a private take.
2. Watch and revisit it in a private archive.
3. Group repeated attempts into a practice thread.
4. Share privately for feedback when outside eyes help.
5. Keep the learner-owned history intact over time.

## Core Surfaces

- `Calendar / library`: learner-owned archive and revisit surface
- `Upload / record`: fast private capture
- `Session detail`: watch, organize, and decide whether to share
- `Review page`: trusted feedback attached to the take

## Product Principles

- Private by default
- Learner-owned archive
- Video-first self-review
- Feedback attached to the artifact
- Repetition over performance
- Trusted sharing over public discovery

## What Was Intentionally Out In v1

- public discovery
- marketplace behavior
- school administration
- public reputation systems
- heavy teacher-first dashboards

## Acceptance Criteria

v1 succeeds when:

- a learner can privately capture a take quickly,
- the take is easy to replay,
- a trusted reviewer can respond without leaving the product,
- feedback remains attached to the source video,
- and repeated takes naturally accumulate into a durable learning history.

## Naming Guide For v1

Preferred language:

- `take`
- `practice thread`
- `private archive`
- `trusted feedback`
- `review link`
- `reviewer`

## Strategic Note

Practica v1 should now be interpreted as the shipped foundation for a private practice mirror.

The later reviewer or teacher workflow layer should deepen that foundation, not replace it.
